# Street Runner 🏃💨

### Outrun the cops. Grab the coins. Beat your best.

**The endless city runner with real 3D characters – free on Android and iOS.**

`Free to play` · `One-thumb controls` · `Works offline` · `No pay-to-win`

Sprint through a neon-lit city at full speed: leap over hurdles, slide under street signs, weave between buses, sports cars and oncoming traffic – and grab every coin on the way. Every single run starts with a police cruiser on your tail. Shake it off, and then it is just you, the street and your own record.

---

## 🚓 The cops are always behind you

The moment you start moving, a red and blue light begins flashing in the streets behind you. The strobe gets closer, the sirens get louder – the only way out is to run faster than the law. Make it, and your screen celebrates with a big **"GOT AWAY"**. Every run opens with a proper chase.

## 🔥 Why players keep coming back

- **🏃 Real 3D runners, not blocky shapes** – Six characters, each built as a full 3D figure with its own outfit: hoodie and cap, cyber visor and thrusters, ninja hood with a flapping headband, golden armour with wings.
- **🛹 A skater who really skates** – Pick Skater Sam and he carves across the lanes on a proper skateboard, wheels spinning, instead of running.
- **👕 Skins that change everything** – Neon glow, blazing flame trails, shimmering gold metal or a green Matrix glitch – your runner, your style.
- **🚌 A city that gets in your way** – City buses and trains blocking whole lanes, hurdles to jump, signs to slide under, trash cans, double barriers and oncoming traffic you have to dodge at speed.
- **🪙 Coins in every pattern** – Golden arcs over hurdles, tunnels of coins under signs, sweeping S-curves through the lanes. Every coin you touch is yours.
- **🧲 Power-ups with real impact** – Pull in every coin around you with the magnet, double your points with the 2X multiplier, or survive one fatal crash inside a shield bubble.
- **🛡️ Get stronger, run further** – Upgrade your magnet, your boost, your jump and your shield in the shop, and start every run with a protective bubble if you like.
- **🏆 Chase your own record** – The moment you pass your personal best, the game tells you with a "NEW HIGH SCORE!" celebration – right in the middle of the run.
- **💛 One more chance** – Crashed? Watch a short video and jump straight back in. Once per run.
- **📱 Play with one thumb** – Swipe to change lanes, jump and slide, or use the keyboard if you prefer.

## ⚡ Power-ups & upgrades

**During a run you will find:**

- 🧲 **Magnet** – sucks in every coin around you
- ⚡ **2X Multiplier** – double score and double coin value
- 🛡️ **Shield Bubble** – absorbs one crash and gives you a short recovery window

**In the shop you can level up:**

- Longer magnet and 2X durations, a wider magnet radius
- A stronger shield, plus a shield that is active from the very first step
- Higher jumps, a rocket headstart, more score per metre and a chance for double coins

## 🎮 How to play

- **Swipe left / right** – change lane
- **Swipe up** – jump
- **Swipe down** – slide (or fast-fall out of the air)
- **Keyboard** – arrow keys or `W`/`A`/`S`/`D`, `Space` to jump, `ESC` or `P` to pause
- **Every run counts** – score, distance and coins are tracked, and your best run stays saved on your device

## 🔐 Your ads, your choice

The game plays completely offline and keeps your progress on your own device – no account, no login. Ads are what keeps it free, and you stay in control: before any ad is requested you are asked for your consent, and you can change or withdraw it any time via the privacy icon in the main menu.
